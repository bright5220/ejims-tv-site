Our story

Small screen. Big energy.

Ejims TV launched on 21 October 2020 with one goal: to make people's WhatsApp status the best part of their day. We share the latest gists, music, sports moments and plugs for brands we believe in.

We're a community first. Viewers come for the good mood, and businesses come because those viewers are real, active people who tap, reply and buy.

More of the story is coming soon: the founder, the name behind "Ejims", and our milestones so far.
